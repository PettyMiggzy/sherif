# Robin Labs — project rules

## Canonical public repository (RULE)
Any time you reference, link, or point anyone at "the repo" / "the lab repo" for Robin Labs
— in docs, chat, integration write-ups, or links handed to third parties (e.g. Uniswap) —
use exactly:

    https://github.com/Robinlabz/Labs

That is the dedicated public Robin Labs repository. Do NOT point people at `PettyMiggzy/sherif`
(it mixes multiple projects and is not the public face of Robin Labs).
