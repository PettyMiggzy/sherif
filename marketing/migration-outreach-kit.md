## Concierge flow (convert a "yes" in ~10 minutes)

When someone bites, do it WITH them, live on a call or in DMs:
1. Get their old contract address. Open robinlab.io/create.html#migrate, paste it, confirm name/ticker/logo imported.
2. If DexScreener has no logo yet, have them upload the image (30 seconds).
3. Walk them through the one launch tx (remind them: no approval, no authority grant, just gas).
4. The moment it's live, YOU send a small buy so the first candle is green, not a flatline.
5. Send them the share card + their coin page link; help them post the relaunch to their own community.
6. Feature them: quote-post the migration from @robinlabzz as Robin's first migration case study.

Aftercare that compounds:
- Check in day 1 and day 3. A founder who feels supported tells other founders.
- If it charts at all, turn it into a testimonial and lead the next DMs with it.
