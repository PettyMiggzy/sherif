1. Most limit-order tools hold your funds until fill. Robin's never touch them. Non-custodial limit orders and DCA, explained in 4 tweets.
2. Limit orders: you sign, the keeper executes when your price hits. Your tokens stay in your wallet the entire time. The keeper can fill your order. It can never move your funds anywhere else.
3. DCA is the same model. Set size and interval, sign once, and the keeper buys on schedule. No deposit into a custody contract, no handing over your stack. You keep the keys the whole way.
4. And every fill clears Robin Guard first: auto-slippage plus pre-sign simulation, so you see the outcome before it lands. Keeper-executed, never custodial. Start here: robinlab.io/swap.html

2. In a presale you wire funds up front and trust a roadmap. The team holds the ETH, the allocations, and the timeline. You're an unsecured creditor hoping liquidity shows up.
3. A Robin fair launch flips it. One transaction seeds real Uniswap v3 liquidity at launch, with a permanent price floor. Nothing to wait for. The market exists the second it's live.
