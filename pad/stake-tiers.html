What is “the pot”?

When someone leaves early, their 15% is split among everyone still staked. But if nobody else is staked at that moment, there is no one to pay — so it goes in the pot and sits there in the open, counted, until there are stakers to hand it to.

The pot has exactly one exit and it leads back to stakers. There is no function in the contract that can send it to a wallet — not the team's, not anyone's. That is enforced by the code, not by a promise.

Add rewards to this pool

Nothing arrives here on its own. When a coin bonds, every unsold token goes into its Bond — the floor and the sell-wall — not into staking. So whatever this pool pays out is whatever gets put in here. It streams to stakers over the reward window rather than landing as a lump, which is what stops someone staking for one block to grab it.
