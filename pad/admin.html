🎯 Anti-snipe blocklist

Works only for a coin whose factory launches with an anti-snipe window, and only inside that ≤300s window: add-only, buy-only, auto-frozen when the window ends, so it can never block a holder's sell. A coin launched by a factory that ships no guard has no blocklist to seed and this will revert.
