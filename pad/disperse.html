Disperse

Send one of your coins to many wallets in a single transaction. Non-custodial: the tokens go straight from your wallet to each recipient - they never touch any middle contract. You pay one approval + one send, no matter how many wallets.

not connected
Amounts are in whole tokens (e.g. 1000 = 1000 coins).

Tip: this is the same engine that powers "split my dev buy" on the create page.
