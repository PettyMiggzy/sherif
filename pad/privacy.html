Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Robin Labs ("Robin Labs", "we", "us") operates a non-custodial launchpad and trading interface for tokens on Robinhood Chain, available at robinlab.io and in our mobile apps. This policy explains what we do and do not collect. In plain terms: we do not run accounts, we never hold your funds, and we never have your private keys or seed phrase.

What we do not collect

  • No usernames, passwords, or user accounts.
  • No private keys, seed phrases, or custody of your assets. You connect your own self-custody wallet, and every transaction is signed by you.
  • No names, emails, or phone numbers are required to use the app.

What is involved in normal use

  • Your wallet address. When you connect a wallet, your public address is used to read balances and to build transactions you sign. Public blockchain addresses and on-chain activity are inherently public on Robinhood Chain and are not private information we control.
  • Coin profiles. If you create a coin, you may optionally submit a logo, description, and social links. These are signed by your wallet and published publicly on your coin page. Do not submit anything you want kept private.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites and apps, our servers may process standard request data (such as IP address and device or browser type) to deliver content and protect against abuse. We do not use this to identify you personally.

Third-party services

To function, the app talks to third parties, each with their own privacy practices we do not control: the wallet you choose, blockchain RPC providers, and data or routing services such as DexScreener, Uniswap, and LI.FI. Your interactions with those services are governed by their policies.

Selling data

We do not sell your data. We do not build advertising profiles.

Children

The app is intended for adults 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new date.
